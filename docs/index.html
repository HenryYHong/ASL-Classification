How it works

Fingerspelling only — one letter at a time, no words, no grammar. MediaPipe finds 21 hand landmarks; a random forest reads the handshape; a state machine decides when a letter was actually signed. J and Z are motion letters, so they need a launch pose held still, then the stroke. Video stays on your device.

Trained on one signer across four sessions: 0.759 on held-out sessions for the static letters, 0.864 for J and Z. Accuracy varies with hand shape and lighting, and M, N and S are the least reliable.
